The change

You already have a system. It just lives in five places.

Most operators run on a monthly grid in Excel, a WhatsApp thread, a receipt book and a folder of scanned licences. It works — until two people update it at once, or a road tax expires quietly.

Instead of

A tab per month

One live calendar for the whole fleet. Drag across the days to book a car. Double-bookings are refused before they happen, not discovered at the counter.

Instead of

A receipt book

The invoice is the booking. Advances and security deposits are tracked separately, every payment is logged with who took it and when, and the month's totals add up on their own.

Instead of

A folder of scans

Licence, road tax, insurance, fitness and lease expiry live on the car. The app tells you what runs out this month and next — before the fine, not after.

Getting it on your phone

Nothing to download from a store.

FleetMate runs in the browser and saves itself to your home screen, so it opens like any other app — with your icon, full screen, no address bar. Once we've set up your account we'll send you the link.

iPhone & iPad

  1. Open the link we send you in Safari. It has to be Safari — other browsers can't do this step.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom — the square with an arrow coming out of it.
  3. Scroll down the list and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add. The icon appears with your other apps.

Android

  1. Open the link in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots at the top right.
  3. Tap Install app, or Add to Home screen if you don't see that.
  4. Confirm. The icon appears on your home screen.

Computer

  1. Open the link in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Look for the small install icon at the right-hand end of the address bar.
  3. Click it, then click Install.
  4. Or just bookmark the link and use it in the browser — it works exactly the same.
